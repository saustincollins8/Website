An exploration of the major religious, social, intellectual and political changes in Western Europe from the late Middle Ages to the close of the 16th century. Particular emphasis is given to the relationship between Italian Humanism and the Protestant Reformation and to the continuity of the movement for Ecclesiastical Reform throughout the period.
