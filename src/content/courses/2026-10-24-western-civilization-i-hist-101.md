This course surveys the social, cultural, political, economic, and religious development of the Western world from its earliest civilizations through the sixteenth century. It examines the formation of distinctive identities among the peoples, societies, states, and empires of the West and analyzes their contributions to what would later be understood as the foundations of the West. The course also investigates the evolving interactions between Western and non-Western societies and assesses the broader global influence of Western civilization. Areas of focus include religion and philosophy, trade and commerce, technology and war, literature and the arts, and gender relations.
